Insurance

Your team is great at insurance. Let's make the paperwork easier.

Policies, claims, underwriting, renewals: there's a lot of writing in insurance. The good news? AI tools can handle the heavy lifting, so your team spends less time on documentation and more time on the work that matters. I'll show you exactly how, step by step.

Let's talk about your team See how training works

Sound familiar? You're not alone.

Many carriers, agencies, and brokerages I talk to face these same challenges. The good news is they're exactly the kind of thing AI is great at helping with.

Underwriting

Submissions piling up

Your underwriters are spending hours reading applications and writing risk summaries, and the queue just keeps growing. AI can help them get through submissions faster, so agents aren't waiting days for quotes.

Claims

Too much time writing things up

Your adjusters do great work in the field, then spend just as long writing it all up. Claims narratives, reserve justifications, status updates. AI can draft those from their notes in much less time.

Renewals

Renewals that never slow down

A steady stream of renewals every month, and each one needs a coverage review, carrier remarketing, a proposal, and client outreach. It's a lot. AI can take the repetitive parts off your team's plate so every client gets the attention they deserve.

Compliance

Keeping documentation consistent

Coverage recommendations, claims decisions, client communications: consistency matters for protecting your team, and regulators such as the AMF (Québec) and FSRA (Ontario) expect fair, well-documented treatment of clients. AI helps create reliable templates and review processes so nothing falls through the cracks.

Great tools are out there. I'll help you pick the right ones.

There are plenty of AI tools built for insurance. The tricky part is knowing which ones fit your team and how to start using them confidently. That's where I come in.

Underwriting & Risk Assessment

Paid

Cytora

Helps your underwriters get structured risk assessments faster. It pulls in submission data, enriches it with outside sources, and delivers a clear picture, so your team can focus on decision-making, not data gathering. Now part of Applied Systems.

Paid

Sixfold

Reads incoming submissions, flags missing information, and checks each risk against your appetite so your team can prioritize the right ones. It works alongside your existing workflows, with no need to change how you operate.

Paid

Planck

Automatically gathers and analyzes business data to give your underwriters a head start. Pre-fills applications and surfaces insights so your team spends less time on research. Now part of Applied Systems.

Claims Processing

Paid

Tractable

Upload photos of damage and Tractable estimates repair costs quickly. It can speed up auto and property settlements. Your adjusters and policyholders both appreciate the faster turnaround.

Paid

Five Sigma (Clive)

A cloud claims platform with an AI claims assistant, Clive, that reads incoming documents, drafts the first notice of loss, checks coverage, and routes each claim to the right handler. Your adjusters keep the final say.

Paid

Snapsheet

Your policyholders snap photos, Snapsheet generates repair estimates, and claims close faster, with a lot less back-and-forth for everyone involved.

Fraud Detection

Paid

FRISS

Works quietly in the background, checking applications, claims, and renewals for anything that doesn't look right. Your team gets a simple score, with no extra work required.

Paid

DataVisor

Spots patterns that are hard for people to catch, like coordinated fraud rings or new schemes. It learns on its own, so it gets better over time without extra setup from your team.

Paid

Shift Technology

Flags suspicious claims early (right at first notice of loss) and explains why, so your team can make informed decisions. Fits right into the claims tools you already use.

Customer Service & Communication

Paid

Hi Marley

A texting platform built specifically for insurance. Your adjusters, agents, and policyholders can communicate via SMS, with smart response suggestions that save time on every conversation.

Paid

Pypestream

Handles the common questions your team fields all day: policy inquiries, billing, certificate requests, even first notice of loss. Your clients get quick answers, and your team gets breathing room.

Freemium

Otter.ai

Records and transcribes your client calls, claim interviews, and team discussions. You get a clean summary and action items after every conversation, so no more scrambling for notes. Get the client’s consent before recording.

Document Processing

Paid

Hyperscience

Reads your policies, endorsements, submissions, and claim forms (even handwritten ones) and pulls out the key information automatically. No more manually hunting through long documents for the details you need.

Paid

Indico Data

Sorts your incoming submissions, correspondence, and claims documents, then pulls out the important details and sends them where they need to go. Less manual sorting, more getting things done.

Paid

Guidewire AI

If you're already on Guidewire, you'll love this: AI features built right into the platform you know. Document analysis, smarter workflows, and helpful predictions, all without switching tools.

Actuarial & Pricing

Paid

Akur8

Takes the heavy math out of rate modeling while keeping everything transparent and explainable. Your pricing team stays in control. AI just makes the process a lot faster.

Paid

Earnix

Helps you find the right price in real time, personalized for each risk and built to support compliance across your lines of business. Your team gets better pricing with less guesswork.

Paid

Duck Creek AI

Makes policy administration smoother with built-in AI that helps with product setup, rating, and issuance. Your team gets helpful analytics and decision support along the way.

Everyday AI & Automation

Freemium

Claude

Perfect for reading long documents. Upload a full policy, compare coverage across carriers, or draft an underwriting memo. Claude handles it all and is surprisingly easy to get started with.

Freemium

ChatGPT

Great for drafting client communications, proposal letters, coverage explanations, and quick research. Most people pick it up fast, which makes it a nice first step for teams new to AI.

Freemium

Microsoft Copilot

If your team already lives in Word, Excel, and Outlook, this is an easy win. Draft policy documents, analyze loss runs, and manage client email with AI help built right into the tools you already use.

Freemium

Zapier

Connects your agency management system to AI tools without any coding. Set up automatic claims documentation, renewal communications, and certificate processing once, and it runs on its own.

Freemium

Make (Integromat)

A visual way to build automations that connect your carrier portals, CRM, email, and AI tools. You can see how everything flows together, and no coding is needed.

Freemium

Canva AI

Makes it easy for anyone on your team to create polished proposals, coverage comparison sheets, and presentations. No design skills needed: just pick a template and go.

I don't sell any of these tools. I just help your team learn how to use the right ones well. Everything is tailored to your lines of business, your workflows, and your compliance needs.
