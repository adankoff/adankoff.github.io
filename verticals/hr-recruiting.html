HR & Recruiting

Give your recruiting team the AI skills that make their day easier

Your team is talented. They just spend too much time on the repetitive stuff: writing JDs, chasing schedules, formatting feedback. I'll show them, step by step, how AI handles those tasks so they can focus on what they do best: finding and connecting with great people.

Sound familiar? You're not alone.

These are the challenges I often hear from HR and recruiting teams. The good news: each one has a straightforward AI solution your team can start using right away.

Your team is stretched thin and the req list keeps growing

When every recruiter is juggling 20-40 open roles, the repetitive work piles up fast: writing JDs, drafting outreach, prepping interviews. AI takes care of that production work so your team has more time for the human side: building relationships and making great hires.

Personalized outreach takes forever (but it's the only kind that works)

You know generic InMails don't get replies. But crafting a unique message for every candidate across every role? There aren't enough hours. AI helps your team write thoughtful, personalized outreach much faster, so candidates actually want to respond.

The handbook is outdated and the policy backlog never shrinks

Between new regulations, performance review cycles, and onboarding updates, there's always more documentation than hours in the day. AI helps your team draft and update policies in much less time and keeps everything consistent so nothing falls through the cracks.

Staying on top of AI compliance feels overwhelming

Ontario's rule on disclosing AI use in job postings, Québec Law 25's notice for automated decisions, and NYC Local Law 144 or Illinois's AI hiring rules if you recruit in the US: there's a lot to keep up with. I'll help your team feel confident using AI the right way: inclusive language, bias checkpoints, audit trails, and proper candidate disclosure. Responsible and effective, together.

Great tools your team will actually enjoy using

I've worked with many of these and know which ones work best for different team setups. In my training, I go beyond the basics: your team will learn how to build real workflows, write effective prompts, and weave these tools into their daily routine.

AI Recruiting & Sourcing

Paid

HireEZ

A sourcing tool that searches over 800M candidate profiles across the web and helps you write personalized outreach sequences. It also gives you a clear picture of what talent pools look like for any role.

Paid

Fetcher

Think of it as a sourcing assistant that gets smarter over time. It learns what kinds of candidates you're looking for and delivers qualified matches straight to your pipeline.

Paid

Humanly

Really helpful for high-volume roles. It handles screening conversations, schedules interviews, and collects candidate info via friendly chat, so your team isn't buried in coordination.

Paid

Paradox (Olivia)

A friendly AI assistant, now part of Workday, that handles candidate communication from start to finish: screening questions, scheduling, offer letters, even onboarding steps. Candidates love the responsive experience.

Paid

Textio

A writing assistant for job posts that helps you spot exclusionary language, suggests better alternatives, and shows how your JDs compare to what's working in the market. Really eye-opening.

Paid

SeekOut

A talent search tool with smart filters for technical skills, diversity sourcing, and finding hidden talent inside your own organization. Especially great for hard-to-fill engineering roles.

AI Resume Screening & Assessment

Paid

Harver (formerly Pymetrics)

Uses short, game-like assessments to understand candidates' strengths and match them to roles without relying on resume keywords alone. A thoughtful way to make screening fairer.

Paid

HiredScore

Now part of Workday, it works inside Workday Recruiting to quietly surface the best-fit candidates and re-engage people already in your pipeline. No extra tabs to manage.

Paid

Eightfold AI

Goes beyond keyword matching to understand candidates' actual skills and career trajectory. Helps your team discover people who are a great fit, even if their resume doesn't use the "right" words.

Paid

Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby

If you're already using one of these, you have more AI built in than you might realize. I'll show your team how to use the smart screening, scheduling, and analytics features that are already there.

AI Interviewing & Feedback

Paid

HireVue

Helps your team run structured video interviews with AI-generated guides and easy scheduling. Especially popular with teams doing high-volume hiring or campus recruiting.

Paid

Metaview

Your team's favorite interview note-taker. It listens, captures everything, and turns it into clean, structured feedback aligned with your scorecard. No more scrambling to write notes after a call.

Paid

BrightHire

Records interviews, pulls out the highlights, and gives your hiring team a complete picture of every conversation. Makes it much easier to compare candidates fairly and make confident decisions. Now part of Zoom.

Freemium

Otter.ai

A simple, reliable way to transcribe interviews, debriefs, and hiring committee meetings. The free version handles basic transcription, and the paid tier adds summaries and action items.

AI HR Operations

Paid

Leena AI

Answers everyday employee questions about leave, benefits, and policies instantly, so your HR team doesn't spend half the day responding to the same questions. Also shows you trends in what people are asking about.

Paid

Moveworks

Now part of ServiceNow, it handles employee requests on the spot: benefits questions, policy lookups, system access, onboarding tasks. Your team gets fewer tickets, and employees get faster answers. Everyone wins.

Paid

Rippling

Brings payroll, benefits, compliance, and device management together in one place with smart automation throughout. Takes care of the employee lifecycle from day one to departure.

Paid

BambooHR

A well-loved HRIS for mid-size teams with helpful AI features for performance reviews, engagement surveys, and reporting. If your team already uses BambooHR, there's probably more it can do for you.

AI Learning & Development

Paid

Degreed

Matches learning content to the skills your people actually need, creates personalized development paths, and shows you how skill-building connects to your workforce goals. Smart and practical.

Paid

Cornerstone AI

Helps you recommend the right learning to the right people, map skills across your organization, match employees to internal opportunities, and plan for what's next.

Paid

Lattice

Makes performance reviews, engagement surveys, goal setting, and comp planning feel a lot more manageable. AI helps with the drafting and analysis so your team can focus on the conversations.

AI Compensation & People Analytics

Freemium

Pave

Gives you real-time comp benchmarking based on live data from connected HRIS systems. Helps your team model offers, check pay equity, and set fair compensation bands with confidence.

Paid

Figures

If your team hires in Europe, this is especially useful. AI-powered benchmarking helps you set competitive pay, model equity packages, and stay ahead of pay transparency requirements.

Paid

Visier

Turns your people data into clear, leadership-ready stories about attrition risk, workforce planning, and diversity. Great for when you need to walk into a board meeting with answers, not spreadsheets.

General AI Tools

Freemium

Claude (Anthropic)

Wonderful for longer HR writing: policies, handbook sections, compliance docs, detailed analysis. You can upload your company context so it stays consistent across everything it helps you draft.

Freemium

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Great for quick drafts: outreach messages, JDs, interview questions, brainstorming. You can even build custom GPTs for tasks your team does over and over, like writing JDs for specific departments.

Freemium

Microsoft Copilot

If your team lives in Microsoft, this is the easiest starting point. Copilot Chat comes with Microsoft 365 and works in Outlook; Copilot inside Word, Excel, and PowerPoint needs a paid Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Draft documents, analyze data, and summarize meetings without leaving the tools you already know.

Automation Platforms

Freemium

Zapier

Connects all your tools together without any coding. For example: a new application automatically triggers an AI screening summary, or a completed interview sends a feedback request. Set it up once and it just runs.

Freemium

Make (formerly Integromat)

Similar to Zapier but lets you build more detailed workflows with branching paths and filters. Great for teams that want to get creative with how they connect their recruiting tools together.

Paid

Notion AI

A lovely workspace for your internal wikis, handbooks, and SOPs. The built-in AI helps with drafting, summarizing, and keeping everything up to date, so your knowledge base stays fresh instead of gathering dust. Full AI features come with the Business plan.
