The tools I'll help your team get comfortable with

There are a lot of AI tools out there. I'll help you cut through the noise by showing your team which ones are worth using, how to get the most out of them, and where to keep a human in the loop.

Research & Analysis

Great tools for market research, competitive intelligence, and making sense of lots of data.

Perplexity

Freemium

Think of it as a research assistant that always cites its sources. Your team will love it for market research, industry trends, and competitive landscapes. It pulls from current web sources and organizes what it finds.

AlphaSense

Paid

A powerful way to search across financial filings, earnings transcripts, broker research, and news, all in one place. Especially helpful for due diligence and strategy engagements.

Tegus (now part of AlphaSense)

Paid

Makes it easy to pull insights from expert interviews, earnings calls, and industry data. If your team does a lot of expert network calls, this can save hours of manual note-taking.

Visible Alpha

Paid

Now part of S&P Global. Pulls detailed financial data points from analyst models so your team doesn't have to dig through reports manually. Really handy for benchmarking and market sizing work.

Julius AI

Freemium

Upload a spreadsheet, ask a question in plain English, and get charts and analysis back. No coding needed, so it's great for quick data exploration when you need answers fast.

NotebookLM

Freemium

Google’s research notebook. Upload interview notes, reports, and source documents, then ask questions across all of them. Every answer points back to its source, which makes it a strong fit for research synthesis.

Presentations & Deliverables

Tools that help your team build decks, documents, and visuals faster, without sacrificing quality.

Microsoft Copilot

Paid

AI built right into PowerPoint, Excel, and Word, the tools your team already uses every day. It can draft decks, analyze data, and write documents. For most firms, this is the easiest place to start.

Gamma

Freemium

Give it a text brief and it builds a polished, good-looking deck in minutes. Way faster than starting from scratch in PowerPoint, and you can always export to PPT for final touches.

Beautiful.ai

Paid

Automatically formats your slides as you add content, so everything looks consistent and professional. Perfect if your team wants great-looking decks without needing a designer.

Canva

Freemium

Canva’s AI design tools turn an outline into on-brand slides, one-pagers, and visuals. Really shines for client-facing stories and thought leadership pieces when there's no designer on the team.

Gemini in Google Workspace

Paid

If your firm runs on Google Workspace, Gemini works inside Sheets, Docs, and Slides. Ask questions about your data in plain language and get answers. Great for quick models and analyses your team can share.

Miro AI

Paid

AI built into Miro's whiteboarding tools. It can generate diagrams, organize sticky notes into themes, and summarize workshop outcomes. A real time-saver for teams that run a lot of workshops.

Writing & Communication

Tools that help with drafting, synthesis, analysis, and keeping clients in the loop, so writing never holds up the work.

Claude

Freemium

Excellent for long-form analysis, research synthesis, and applying your firm's frameworks. Handles complex, multi-step reasoning really well. Enterprise tier available to keep client data private.

ChatGPT

Freemium

A great all-rounder for quick drafting, brainstorming, data interpretation, and client communication. Most people find it easy to pick up, and the enterprise tier keeps your data separate.

Jasper

Paid

Lets you set your firm's voice and tone, then helps everyone write in it consistently. Helpful for thought leadership, marketing materials, and keeping proposals sounding like they came from the same firm.

Otter.ai

Freemium

Automatically transcribes client interviews, workshops, and team meetings in real time. The transcripts feed right into your synthesis workflow, so nothing gets lost between the conversation and the deliverable.

Notion AI

Paid

If your team uses Notion, you'll love this. It summarizes your notes, drafts new content, and answers questions about your engagement wikis and research databases. Keeps everything organized and searchable.

Tableau Agent / Power BI Copilot

Paid

Ask questions about your data in plain language and get clear, shareable charts back. If your engagements involve a lot of data, these tools make it much easier to tell the story visually.

Automation & Workflow

Once your team is comfortable with individual tools, these help connect everything together, so repetitive steps happen automatically.

Zapier

Freemium

Connects your apps together without any coding. Set up things like automatic research alerts, proposal drafting triggers, or engagement onboarding steps, and let them run on their own.

Make

Freemium

Similar to Zapier but handles more complex, multi-step workflows. If your team wants to automate entire production pipelines, from research to deliverable, this is a great option.

Monday AI

Paid

AI features built into Monday.com's project management. It can draft project plans, summarize team updates, and flag timeline risks early. Helpful for keeping engagements on track without extra overhead.

I've thought about the tricky parts so you don't have to

Adopting AI in consulting isn't just about picking the right tools. There are real questions about client trust, quality, and how you run your business. I'll help you navigate all of it.

Keeping client data safe

Your clients trust you with sensitive information. I'll help you set up enterprise-grade tools with proper data isolation from day one, in line with Canadian privacy law (Law 25 in Québec, PIPEDA federally), so you can use AI confidently without compromising that trust.

AI drafts, your team perfects

AI gives you a strong first draft, but your team's expertise is what makes it great. I build a clear review workflow: AI drafts, consultant refines, manager reviews, partner approves. Quality stays high.

Billing and transparency

How do you bill for AI-assisted work? What do you tell clients? These are new questions, and the answers are still evolving. I'll help you build practical policies that feel right for your firm and your clients.
