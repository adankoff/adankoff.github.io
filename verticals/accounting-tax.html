Busy season doesn't have to mean burnout

January through April is intense. I get it. AI can draft research memos, generate workpaper narratives, and write client communications in minutes. Your CPAs still make the calls. They just don't have to write every first draft from scratch.

All those letters and emails? AI can draft them.

Advisory letters, engagement letters, status updates, year-end planning letters. Every engagement comes with a pile of writing. AI drafts them using your templates and your firm's voice, so you review and send instead of writing from scratch.

Documentation that writes itself (almost)

Financial statement notes, workpaper narratives, management letter findings, audit documentation. These follow predictable patterns, and that's exactly where AI shines. It creates solid first drafts your team reviews and approves. Same professional standards, a lot less tedium.

Do more with the great team you already have

Finding and keeping good people is tough right now. AI helps your existing team go further. A senior with AI support can take on much more of the documentation work, and the output is often more consistent, too.

Your professional standards come first. Always.

Every training option covers how to use AI responsibly in accounting, including client confidentiality under Québec's Law 25 and PIPEDA. I take this seriously: privacy and compliance rules are built into each workflow, and we review them with your compliance lead.

CPA Canada / AICPA standards

AI output still needs to meet professional quality standards and your provincial CPA order's code of ethics, and I'll show your team exactly how. You'll learn review workflows that help keep AI-assisted deliverables meeting those standards and defensible.

Due diligence: CRA, Revenu Québec, Circular 230

Due diligence requirements don't change just because AI helped draft the memo, whether the file goes to the CRA, Revenu Québec, or the IRS under Circular 230. That's why I build in verification steps and source-checking at every stage, so your team can use AI with confidence.

Practice inspection and peer review readiness

AI-assisted workpapers need to be documented and reviewable, just like any other work product, whether for your CPA order's practice inspection or a US peer review. I'll teach your team documentation practices that make everything transparent and easy for reviewers to follow.
