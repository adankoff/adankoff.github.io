Construction

Give your team the easiest way to handle construction paperwork

RFIs, dailies, change orders, submittals, safety reports: your team already knows this stuff inside and out. AI just helps them get it on paper faster. I'll show your crew how to turn field notes into polished documents in minutes, so they can spend more time doing what they're actually great at.

Sound familiar? You're not alone.

Construction teams often tell me the same thing: the paperwork never ends. Here's the good news: AI handles the tedious parts so your people can focus on the work they love.

Too Much Paperwork

Your PMs became project managers, not report writers

Daily reports, RFIs, change orders, status updates: your PMs can spend a big part of their week on documentation. AI helps them draft these from quick voice notes and field data, so they get back to actually managing projects.

Field Notes Gap

So much gets seen on-site but never written down

Your supers know everything happening in the field, but who has time to document all of it? With voice-to-document AI, they can talk through what they saw and get a clean report back. No typing, no sitting down at a desk after a long day.

Better Records

Documentation that's thorough, even on the busiest days

We all know that RFIs and change orders written at 7pm after a 12-hour day aren't anyone's best work. AI helps your team produce more complete, consistent documentation, so your records are solid whenever you need them.

Consistency

Same great quality, no matter who's writing it

Right now, documentation quality depends on who's doing it and what kind of day they're having. AI templates and workflows give everyone on your team a head start, so reports come out polished and consistent across every project.

Great tools your team will actually want to use

There are a lot of AI tools out there, and it's hard to know where to start. I've done the homework. Here are the ones that work well for construction, and I'll help your team get comfortable with whichever ones fit best.

Built specifically for construction teams

Scheduling & Planning

ALICE Technologies

Think of it as a scheduling assistant that can explore millions of options to find the best sequence for your project. Already trusted on huge projects worldwide, and the conversational AI feature makes it easy to ask questions about your schedule in plain English.

Paid
Progress Tracking

OpenSpace

Walk your site with a 360 camera and OpenSpace handles the rest. It maps everything to your plans and tracks progress automatically. It's a simple way to build a visual record of your project without extra paperwork.

Paid
Progress Tracking

Buildots

Clip a 360 camera to a hardhat and Buildots does the rest. It compares what's actually happening on-site to your BIM models and schedule. A great early warning system that spots issues before they become headaches.

Paid
Progress Tracking

Doxel

Doxel compares LiDAR and camera scans of your site against the BIM model and schedule, so you get a clear, honest picture of where every trade stands without walking every floor with a clipboard.

Paid
Project Management

Procore

Chances are your team already knows Procore. The good news: their newer AI features for document management, RFI tracking, and analytics mean you can start getting AI benefits without learning a whole new system.

Paid
Safety

Newmetrix (now part of Oracle)

Upload site photos and Newmetrix spots potential safety hazards: missing PPE, scaffolding issues, blocked exits. Oracle now sells it alongside Aconex and Primavera, and it helps you understand which projects need extra attention, so your safety team can focus where it matters most.

Paid
Scheduling

nPlan

nPlan draws on data from millions of past construction schedules to show you where your timeline might be at risk, and what you can do about it. Really helpful for complex projects where you want an extra set of eyes on the schedule.

Paid
Design & Build

Autodesk Construction Cloud

If your architects and engineers are already in the Autodesk world, this is a natural fit. It adds AI-powered clash detection and risk insights across the design-build lifecycle, all within the tools your team already knows.

Paid
Plan Review

Bluebeam Revu

A go-to for plan markup and review, now with AI features that speed up measurement, quantity takeoff, and document comparison. If your team does plan reviews, Bluebeam makes the process noticeably smoother.

Paid

Takeoff and estimating, minus the tedium

Takeoff

Togal.AI

Togal handles much of the takeoff work for you: detecting, measuring, and labeling spaces on your drawings. What used to take hours of manual measurement can take much less time. Your estimators may wonder how they lived without it.

Paid
Takeoff & Estimating

Kreo

Kreo reads your 2D plans, pulls out quantities, and feeds them straight into your estimates, so there are fewer manual counting mistakes. Bids often come together much faster than doing takeoff by hand.

Paid
Takeoff

PlanSwift

A longtime favorite for trades and estimators who want fast, flexible quantity takeoff. PlanSwift keeps things straightforward with customizable formulas, and its Takeoff Boost AI tools (auto takeoff, auto count, auto scale) make an already quick workflow even faster.

Paid

Everyday AI tools your team can start using today

Document Intelligence

Claude

Really shines when you need to dig through long documents: specs, contracts, submittals. You can feed it a 200+ page document and ask questions in plain English. I love it for RFI research, spec review, and pulling out the contract clauses you need.

Free tier available
Documentation

ChatGPT

Probably the easiest place for your team to start. Great for drafting RFIs, daily reports, bid narratives, and sub communication. The voice input feature is especially handy. Your supers can talk into their phone right from the field.

Free tier available
Office Integration

Microsoft Copilot

If your company runs on Microsoft 365, you'll feel right at home. Copilot works inside the Excel, Word, Outlook, and Teams you already use: drafting emails, building spreadsheet formulas, summarizing meetings. No new tools to learn.

Paid (with M365)

Helpful extras that tie everything together

Transcription

Otter.ai

Hit record during meetings, site walks, or owner calls and Otter transcribes everything automatically, plus gives you summaries and action items. It's the easiest first step toward making field reporting painless.

Freemium
Automation

Zapier / Make

These connect your tools together without any coding. For example: a new RFI in Procore automatically triggers an AI draft response, or a daily log entry gets turned into a formatted report. Set it up once, and it runs on its own.

Freemium
Knowledge Base

Notion AI

A great home for your project SOPs, lessons learned, and company knowledge. The AI search means anyone on your team can find answers quickly, and it helps keep documentation practices consistent across the whole organization.

Paid (Business plan)

Let's find the right fit

Pick the level that feels right for your team

Whether you want a quick intro or a full hands-on engagement, there's a great starting point here. Each level builds on the last, but you can jump in wherever makes sense. I'll meet you where you are.

Level 1

See What's Possible: A Friendly Introduction

1 hour · Presentation with live demos · 10-100+ attendees

Just come and watch, no laptops needed. I'll show your team real examples: an RFI drafted from a field photo, a daily report from voice notes, a change order narrative in minutes. By the end, your team will be excited about what's possible, not intimidated by it.

  • Watch an RFI go from field photo to finished draft in a few minutes
  • See a daily report generated from simple voice notes: weather, manpower, equipment, issues
  • Watch a bid narrative come together from project specs
  • Honest conversation about what AI does well and where it still needs a human touch
  • You'll take home a time savings calculator and a handy tool recommendation card
Best for: GC company meetings, CCA, ACQ, and APCHQ events, project kickoffs, conference sessions
Level 2

Try It Yourself: Guided Hands-On Workshop

2 hours · Guided hands-on workshop · 8-20 participants

This is where your team gets to try it themselves with me right there to help. Everyone will draft RFIs, generate daily reports, and set up AI on their phone for field use. You'll leave with 15 ready-to-use prompts and a fun 7-day challenge to build the habit.

  • I'll explain which tool works best for what: Claude for long docs, ChatGPT for quick drafts
  • Draft 3 RFIs and a change order justification together, step by step
  • Generate a daily report from sample field notes and see how easy it is
  • Set up AI on your phone so you can use it right from the jobsite
  • Take home a 15-prompt Construction Prompt Pack and ready-made templates
Best for: PMs, superintendents, estimators, project engineers
Level 3 · Most Popular

Make It Yours: Set Up AI for Your Projects

4 hours (half-day) · Configuration workshop · 6-15 participants

Bring your project specs, your templates, and your Procore login. We'll work together to set up AI that knows your projects: your document formats, your workflows, your way of doing things. You'll leave with everything configured and ready to use the next day.

  • Set up AI workspaces loaded with your project specs, scope, and contract terms
  • Build a template library together: RFIs by trade, dailies in your format, change orders, safety docs
  • Connect your Procore and PM tools so AI drafts and reports flow automatically
  • Put together a sensible AI documentation policy your team can follow with confidence
  • Walk away with a configured workspace, 5+ templates, 2+ integrations, and a 30-day plan
Best for: Teams who've tried AI a bit and are ready to make it part of their routine
Level 4

Build Your Workflows: A Full-Day Deep Dive

7 hours (full day) · Workflow building workshop · 6-12 participants

Spend a day with me mapping out how AI fits into every phase of your projects, from pre-construction through closeout. We'll build workflows together for daily reports, RFIs, change orders, safety docs, and closeout packages, then document everything as SOPs your whole team can follow.

  • Map out every document type across your project lifecycle together
  • Pre-construction: streamline contract review, submittal logs, and safety plans
  • Daily operations: set up voice-to-report, RFI management, and change order workflows
  • Safety docs: toolbox talks, JSAs, incident reports, and CNESST or provincial OHS compliance (OSHA on US sites) made easier
  • Closeout: punch lists, as-builts, O&M summaries, and warranty docs, all streamlined
  • You'll leave with 3+ complete workflows, documented SOPs, and a rollout plan
Best for: PMs, superintendents, project executives, operations managers
Level 5 · Two-day intensive

Your Whole Team, Your Real Projects

2 days (14-16 hours) · Intensive hands-on with real projects · 8-25 participants

Bring everyone (PMs, supers, PEs, estimators, safety, admin) along with your real projects and your actual documentation backlog. I'll work alongside your team for two days, and by the end, everyone will have the tools and workflows that can give them hours back each week. It's a great experience and often the fastest way to get your whole team on board.

  • Day 1: Get everyone set up and tackle your real documentation backlog together
  • Day 1: PMs draft overdue RFIs, supers catch up on dailies, estimators knock out bid narratives
  • Day 2: Fine-tune workflows by project type and build your company AI playbook
  • Day 2: Connect workflows across roles, create an onboarding guide, and map out a 90-day plan
  • You'll leave with real docs produced, every project configured, and a playbook your whole team can follow
Best for: GCs and construction companies ready to bring their whole team along for the ride
