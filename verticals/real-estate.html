Sound familiar? You're not alone.

These are the everyday frustrations I hear from agents and brokers. The good news: each one has a simple, practical AI solution you can start using right away.

Marketing

Listing content eats up your evening

Every property needs a unique description, social posts, email blasts, and flyer copy. That's a lot of writing. AI can draft all of it in much less time, and you just polish the parts that need your personal touch.

Follow-up

Leads slip away while you're busy

You're at a showing when a hot lead comes in. By the time you respond, they've moved on. AI can send a warm, personalized reply the moment a lead arrives, so no one falls through the cracks.

Transactions

Transaction details keep you up at night

Checklists, deadline tracking, status updates, inspection summaries, closing prep: every deal has a hundred moving pieces. AI can help you stay on top of all of it without the mental load.

Retention

Staying in touch with past clients is hard

You know referrals come from relationships, but keeping up with hundreds of past clients feels impossible. AI makes it surprisingly easy to stay in touch, thoughtfully and consistently, without it feeling robotic.

Great tools already exist. Let me introduce you.

I'm not selling any of these tools. I just know them really well and can help you figure out which ones are right for your business. Here's a taste of what's out there.

Property Valuation & Market Analysis

These tools help you price with confidence. Think of them as a smart research assistant that pulls comps and market data so you can focus on the conversation with your client.

HouseCanary

Paid

Gives you solid automated valuations, market forecasts, and rental analysis. Popular with brokerages and investors who want to walk into pricing conversations well-prepared.

Zillow Zestimate

Free

Cross-border buyers and US investors are already looking at Zestimates (they cover US homes only). Knowing how they work, and where they're off, makes you the smartest person in the room during pricing discussions.

JLR (Equifax)

Paid

The go-to property data source in Québec: sales history from the land registry, municipal assessments, and market value estimates. Great for walking into a pricing conversation with the numbers already pulled.

Reonomy

Paid

If you're in commercial real estate, this one's a gem. Helps you find properties, track ownership data, and source deals. It's like having a research analyst on your team.

CompStak

Paid

Really helpful for commercial comps: lease and sales data crowdsourced from other brokers. Makes comparable analysis much easier and more accurate.

ATTOM

Paid

Great for digging into neighborhood trends, risk factors, and market patterns. Helps you answer the detailed questions that make clients trust your expertise.

Lead Generation & AI CRM

These tools help you find the right people at the right time and follow up consistently, so you never lose a lead just because you were busy.

Offrs

Paid

Helps you spot likely sellers before they even list. It's like having a crystal ball for your farm area. You'll know who to reach out to and when.

SmartZip

Paid

Combines smart seller predictions with automated outreach. Set up your farm area and let it run. It handles the marketing while you handle the conversations.

Ylopo AI

Paid

Handles your digital marketing and keeps leads warm with texts and even phone calls. It's like having a dedicated follow-up assistant working around the clock for you.

Follow Up Boss

Paid

A fan-favorite CRM that keeps your follow-up organized and automatic. Connects with most lead sources, and I can help you set up smart workflows that do the heavy lifting.

BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE) / Lofty

Paid

All-in-one platforms that do a lot of the thinking for you: lead scoring, smart campaigns, website, the works. Especially popular with teams and brokerages.

HubSpot CRM

Freemium

A great starting point if you're an individual agent. The free CRM keeps your contacts, emails, and follow-up tasks in one place, and paid tiers add AI drafting and automated email sequences. Easy to learn, too.

Virtual Staging & Visual Content

You don't need a photographer or a graphic designer for every listing anymore. These tools let you create beautiful staging, flyers, and presentations quickly.

Virtual Staging AI

Paid

Upload a photo of an empty room and watch it fill with beautiful furniture and decor. Multiple styles to choose from, and results come back in seconds. Way more affordable than physical staging.

REimagineHome

Freemium

Redesign rooms in different styles, swap out furniture, or stage empty spaces, all from a photo. There's a free tier so you can try it before committing.

Apply Design

Paid

Perfect for helping buyers see the potential. Show them what a property could look like with new finishes, different layouts, or fresh furniture. It's a real conversation starter.

Canva AI

Freemium

You've probably heard of Canva. Their AI features make it even easier to create polished flyers, social posts, and listing presentations. Tons of real estate templates to start from.

Gamma / Beautiful.ai

Freemium

Need a listing presentation or buyer consultation deck? Just feed in your property details and get a professional-looking presentation quickly. Your clients will be impressed.

Midjourney / ChatGPT images

Paid / Freemium

Want lifestyle imagery or neighborhood visuals for your marketing? These tools can create custom images that bring a property's story to life. Especially handy for new construction.

Property Management AI

Managing properties means juggling tenant requests, leases, and maintenance all day long. These tools take the repetitive parts off your plate so you can focus on what matters.

AppFolio AI

Paid

A property management favorite. It handles leasing inquiries, routes maintenance requests, and even helps with rent pricing. If you manage a portfolio, this one's worth a look.

Buildium AI Features

Paid

Makes tenant screening, maintenance routing, and communications much smoother. Whether you're a landlord or a property manager, it takes a lot of the back-and-forth off your hands.

EliseAI

Paid

Answers leasing inquiries by text, email, and phone, and books tours automatically. Especially popular with multifamily properties where the inquiry volume can be overwhelming.

Document & Transaction Management

Contracts, deadlines, compliance: the paperwork side of every deal. These tools help you stay organized and catch things before they become problems.

SkySlope

Paid

Reviews your transaction documents and flags anything missing or incomplete before it becomes a problem. Like having a detail-oriented assistant double-checking everything for you.

Dotloop

Paid

Keeps your deals moving from offer to close with built-in e-signatures and smart workflows. Connects with the CRM you already use, so everything stays in one place.

InstanetForms (OACIQ)

Paid

If you broker in Québec, this is where the mandatory OACIQ forms live, available through Centris. AI won't fill them in for you, but it can gather the facts from your file and double-check the finished form against it.

General AI & Automation Platforms

These are your everyday AI companions. They help with writing, emails, documents, and connecting all your other tools together. Most of my clients start here.

ChatGPT

Freemium

Your go-to for listing descriptions, client emails, social posts, and marketing copy. You can even build a custom assistant trained on your brokerage's style and market.

Claude

Freemium

Especially good at reading long documents: contracts, leases, investment memos. You can set up a workspace that remembers your market, your brand, and your preferences.

Microsoft Copilot

Paid

If your brokerage already uses Microsoft, this is a natural fit. AI help right inside Excel, Word, and Outlook, exactly where you're already working.

Google Workspace (Gemini)

Paid

Same idea for Google teams: AI help in Sheets, Docs, and Gmail. If your team lives in Google, these features make your daily tools noticeably smarter.

Zapier

Freemium

Connects all your tools together, no coding needed. Set it up so a new lead automatically gets a personalized email, your listing goes to social media on schedule, and nothing falls through the cracks.

Make (formerly Integromat)

Freemium

Similar to Zapier but with more flexibility. If you like to tinker and want to build custom workflows connecting your tools, this is a great option. The free tier is generous.

Otter.ai

Freemium

Hit record during a client meeting, a showing, or a team huddle, and get a clean summary with action items afterward. No more scribbling notes or forgetting what was discussed.

Wherever you are in real estate, there's a fit

Every part of the industry can benefit, just in different ways. Here's how I typically help teams like yours.

Residential

Individual agents & teams

Listings, follow-ups, client emails: AI helps you get more done without burning out. Agents who try it often get meaningful time back.

Brokerage

Brokerages & firms

Get your whole team on the same page with consistent workflows, brand voice, and compliance. The Two-day intensive (Level 5) is a great way to bring everyone up to speed together.

Commercial

Commercial RE & investment

Deal analysis, due diligence, investor reports, lease review: AI speeds up the research and analysis so you can focus on the relationships.

Property Management

Property management companies

Tenant messages, lease admin, maintenance coordination: all the repetitive tasks that eat up your day. AI handles the volume so you can handle the relationships.

Investment

Real estate investors

Portfolio analysis, market research, deal evaluation: AI helps you move faster and with more confidence on the decisions that matter most.

Compliance

Compliance & privacy

Every level covers the OACIQ guide for licensees using AI, human rights and fair housing rules for listing language, Centris and MLS standards, Law 25 and PIPEDA privacy, and quality review. Privacy and compliance rules are built into each workflow, and we review them with your broker or compliance lead.
