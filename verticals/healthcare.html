I know what's eating up your day

You're not imagining it: the paperwork really has gotten worse. Here's where AI can make the biggest difference, starting right away.

Documentation

Those late nights catching up on charts

You shouldn't have to choose between finishing notes and being home for dinner. AI scribes listen during your visits and draft your notes for you, so after clinic you just review and sign off. Many providers can get much of that evening time back.

Prior Auth

The prior auth headache

I know, prior auths are exhausting. All that time on hold, writing letters, chasing approvals. AI can draft those authorization letters for you using the clinical details already in the chart, and write appeal letters in much less time. Your staff will thank you.

Communication

The never-ending inbox

Patient portal messages pile up faster than you can answer them, and so many are the same questions. AI can draft thoughtful, personalized responses for you to review, create easy-to-read patient handouts, and flag the messages that need your attention first.

Compliance

Quality reports that almost write themselves

Accreditation reports, quality measures (CMS in the US), compliance narratives, incident reports: it's important work, but it doesn't have to be tedious. AI can pull from your structured data and draft these documents for you, so your team can focus on actually improving care instead of describing it.

Great tools are already out there, and I'll help you pick the right ones

There are a lot of AI tools for healthcare now, and it can feel overwhelming. Don't worry: I'll walk your team through which ones fit your practice, how to set them up, and how to use them safely.

Clinical Documentation

Paid

Microsoft Dragon Copilot

Microsoft's ambient documentation assistant (it replaced DAX Copilot and Dragon Medical One) quietly listens during your patient visits and writes up structured notes right inside your EHR. It works with Epic and other systems, and it's a common choice for larger health systems.

Paid

Abridge

Abridge listens to your conversations and creates notes in real time, linking them back to the clinical evidence. It's especially popular with academic medical centers and large group practices, and it adapts nicely to different specialties.

Paid

DeepScribe

Built specifically for specialty practices. What's nice about DeepScribe is that it learns how you like your notes written and adapts to your style over time. A great fit if you're in a multi-specialty group.

Paid

Suki AI

A voice-first assistant that keeps up with your fastest clinic days. Just talk, and Suki handles your notes, suggests codes, and helps you navigate the EHR without slowing down.

Paid

Nabla

Think of Nabla as a helpful copilot that does a little bit of everything: it writes your notes, suggests follow-up actions, and even drafts patient messages based on what happened in the visit.

Paid

Epic AI / Oracle Health (Cerner) AI

If you're already on Epic or Cerner, these built-in AI features are worth exploring. No extra vendor to manage, and often no separate privacy agreement to negotiate. The features are still growing, but the convenience of having it right inside your existing system is hard to beat.

Medical Coding and Billing

Paid

Fathom

Fathom reads through your clinical documentation and suggests the right codes, complete with supporting evidence. It's a real time-saver for your coding team, and it helps catch things that might otherwise get missed.

Paid

Nym Health

Nym Health handles the heavy lifting of turning clinical text into accurate billable codes. If your team processes a high volume of claims, this one can really take the pressure off.

Paid

AGS Health AI

AGS covers your whole billing journey, from coding to predicting denials to writing appeals. If you want one tool that helps across the entire revenue cycle, this is a solid choice.

Patient Engagement and Communication

Paid

Hyro

Hyro answers your patients' most common questions (appointment scheduling, prescription refills, FAQs) so your front desk and call center staff can focus on the patients who need a human touch.

Paid

Luma Health

Luma helps keep your schedule full and your patients on track. It manages waitlists, fills last-minute cancellations, and sends friendly reminders that can help reduce no-shows.

Paid

Notable Health

Notable gets the paperwork done before your patient even walks through the door: registration, insurance verification, and clinical intake, all handled ahead of time so visits start smoothly.

Diagnostics and Clinical Decision Support

Paid

Viz.ai

When every minute counts (stroke, PE, and other emergencies), Viz.ai analyzes imaging and alerts your care team right away. It's FDA-cleared and already helping hospitals catch critical findings faster.

Paid

Aidoc

Aidoc works alongside your radiologists, flagging critical findings so urgent cases move to the top of the worklist. It plugs right into your PACS and helps your team catch what matters most, faster.

Paid

PathAI

PathAI gives your pathology team a helpful second set of eyes, assisting with tissue classification, biomarker detection, and quality checks so diagnoses are more confident and consistent.

Scheduling and Operations

Paid

Qventus

Qventus helps your hospital run more smoothly, from patient flow and bed management to surgical scheduling and discharge planning. It helps spot bottlenecks before they become problems.

Paid

LeanTaaS

LeanTaaS makes scheduling smarter for your ORs, infusion centers, and inpatient beds. Less waiting for patients, less wasted time for your team. It's a win for everyone.

General-Purpose AI Tools

These are great for everyday tasks that don't involve patient data: writing patient education handouts, reviewing literature, drafting policies, prepping for meetings. Some offer enterprise plans with a data-processing agreement (a BAA in the US) when you're ready to go further.

Free tier available

Claude (Anthropic)

Especially helpful for reviewing long documents, synthesizing research, and drafting policies. You can set up specialty-specific workspaces using its Projects feature. An enterprise plan with stronger privacy protections is available too.

Free tier available

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

A great all-rounder for drafting patient communications, creating education materials, and handling admin writing. For anything involving patient data, there's an enterprise tier with stronger privacy terms (and a BAA for US organizations).

Copilot Chat included with Microsoft 365

Microsoft Copilot

If your team already uses Word, Outlook, and Teams, Copilot feels like a natural addition. Copilot Chat and Copilot in Outlook come with Microsoft 365; Copilot inside Word, Excel, and PowerPoint needs a paid Microsoft 365 Copilot license.

Automation Platforms

These tools help your different systems work together, connecting your scheduling, EHR, communication, and billing tools so information flows automatically instead of being copied by hand.

Freemium

Zapier

No coding required: Zapier connects your apps so things happen automatically. Healthcare teams use it for appointment reminders, form routing, and notifications. Keep patient data out of it, though: Zapier doesn't support health information or sign BAAs.

Freemium

Make (formerly Integromat)

Make gives you a visual way to build more detailed workflows, great when you need multi-step processes like intake form handling, referral routing, or pulling together documents from different sources. Get written confirmation from Make before any patient data goes through it.

Freemium

Microsoft Power Automate

If your organization runs on Microsoft, Power Automate fits right in with Teams, SharePoint, and Dynamics. It's a natural way to start automating without adding yet another vendor to the mix.

Privacy and compliance, built into every workflow.

I know privacy is always on your mind, and it should be: PIPEDA, Québec's Law 25 and its health-information act (Law 5), Ontario's PHIPA, and HIPAA if you serve US patients. In Québec, that also means RAMQ requirements for insured services and the Collège des médecins' ethics obligations. That's why I build privacy and compliance rules into each workflow and review them with your compliance lead. I'll show you clearly what's safe to do today with general tools and what needs an enterprise data-processing agreement (a BAA in the US). No shortcuts. Just straightforward guidance so your team can adopt AI with confidence.
