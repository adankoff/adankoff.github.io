Sound familiar? You're not alone.

These are the things I often hear from legal teams. The good news: every one of them is very solvable.

"People are using AI, but we don't have a plan"

Your team is probably already experimenting with ChatGPT for memos and summaries. That's natural. You just need a simple framework so everyone's on the same page about what's okay and what's not. I'll help you put that in place.

Research takes longer than it should

Those long research blocks on Westlaw and Lexis? AI can often shorten them considerably. That's time your team gets back for the work that actually needs a human: client relationships, strategy, and deeper analysis.

Document review eats up everyone's time

Contract review, due diligence, document processing: it adds up fast. AI is really good at doing a fast first pass, so your lawyers can focus on reviewing the highlights instead of reading every page. It's a much better use of everyone's day.

"Are we doing this the right way?"

Between guidance from the Barreau du Québec, the Chambre des notaires, and the Law Society of Ontario (plus the ABA and state bars if you work cross-border), and clients asking about your AI policies, it's natural to feel unsure. The rules are actually more straightforward than they seem, and I'll walk you through exactly what applies to your practice so you can feel confident.

Great tools are out there. I'll help you pick the right ones.

I don't sell any of these tools. I just know them really well and I'll help your team find the ones that fit your practice, your budget, and the way you already work.

Legal Research Tools

Paid

CoCounsel

Thomson Reuters' AI assistant built specifically for legal work. Handles research, document review, and contract analysis. It's one of the most established platforms in this space, so there's a strong community around it.

Paid

Lexis+ AI

If your team already uses Lexis, this is a natural next step. Its Protégé assistant (available in Canada) gives you AI-powered answers grounded in Lexis content with linked citations, right inside the workflow you already know.

Paid

Harvey

Built from the ground up for law firms and trained on legal data. Firms like A&O Shearman and PwC use it. A strong option if you want something purpose-built for your world.

Paid

Westlaw with CoCounsel

Already on Westlaw? Westlaw Edge Canada with CoCounsel adds AI-assisted research, litigation analytics, and brief analysis right where you work. A nice upgrade to tools your team already knows.

Contract Review & Drafting

Paid

Kira (Litera)

Wonderful for due diligence and M&A work. It reads through large stacks of contracts and pulls out the provisions that matter, saving your team a lot of manual review.

Paid

Ironclad AI

Helps your team manage contracts from start to finish, with smart redlining suggestions and clause extraction along the way. Great for legal and procurement teams working together.

Paid

Spellbook

Lives right inside Microsoft Word, which makes it easy to adopt. It suggests clauses, flags potential risks, and helps draft language as you work, with no switching between apps. Built in Toronto.

Paid

Leah (formerly ContractPodAi)

Covers the full contract lifecycle, from extracting key terms to tracking obligations and scoring risk across your portfolio. A solid choice for teams managing lots of agreements.

Freemium

Genie AI

A great place to start if you want to explore AI drafting without a big commitment. Free templates get you going, and the premium tier adds custom clause generation when you're ready.

Paid

Docusign CLM (formerly Lexion)

Lexion is now part of Docusign’s contract management suite. It handles tracking, renewals, and obligation management quietly in the background, alongside the Word and e-signature tools you already use.

E-Discovery

Paid

Relativity aiR

If your team uses Relativity, their AI layer is a huge time-saver. It handles document classification, privilege review, and relevance coding, the tasks that usually take the longest.

Paid

Everlaw AI

A cloud-based platform that makes it easy for your whole litigation team to collaborate. Smart review, predictive coding, and investigation tools all in one place.

Paid

DISCO AI

Takes the heavy lifting out of document review for discovery. It finds the key documents fast and helps your team get to case preparation much sooner.

Practice Management & Billing Made Easier

Paid

Clio

One of the most popular practice management platforms, and it was built in Vancouver. It's easy to use and covers scheduling, billing, tasks, and client intake. Its AI features (Clio Duo and Clio Work) make the day-to-day even smoother.

Paid

PracticePanther

A favorite among small and mid-size firms. Billing, calendaring, and workflow automation are all built in, so your team doesn't need to juggle separate tools.

Paid

Smokeball AI

Quietly captures your time in the background and writes billing narratives for you. If you've ever lost billable hours because you forgot to track them, this one's worth a look.

Paid

TimeSolv

Focused squarely on billing and time tracking, with AI that helps write narratives and create invoices. Simple, reliable, and one less thing to worry about at month-end.

Everyday AI Assistants

Freemium

Claude

My top recommendation for legal work. It can read and analyze very long documents (full contracts, lengthy memos, large case files), which makes it especially helpful for the kind of work lawyers do every day.

Freemium

ChatGPT

Great for quick drafts, brainstorming, client emails, and general writing. You can also set up custom versions tailored to your practice area. I'll show your team how.

Paid

Microsoft Copilot

If your firm runs on Microsoft, Copilot brings AI right into Word, Outlook, and Excel. No new apps to learn: it just makes the tools you already use a little smarter.

Handy Automation Tools

Freemium

Zapier

Connects your tools so they talk to each other, no coding needed. Things like turning intake forms into new matters or calendar events into prep reminders happen automatically.

Freemium

Make

Similar to Zapier but with more flexibility for complex workflows. If you need multi-step automations across several tools, Make handles it beautifully with a visual builder.

Freemium

Otter.ai

Records and transcribes your meetings, depositions, and hearings in real time. Works with Zoom and Teams, and gives you searchable transcripts with helpful AI summaries.

Not sure where to begin? Here's my suggestion.

Solo & small firms

1-10 lawyers. Level 2 or Level 3 is a great fit. You'll build personal AI workflows that give you more capacity without adding headcount, and you'll get time back on non-billable work like intake, admin, and billing.

Mid-size firms

11-100 lawyers. Level 3 or Level 4 works well here. I'll help you get everyone on the same page with consistent tools, quality standards, and clear documentation that the whole firm can follow.

Large firms & in-house teams

100+ lawyers or corporate legal departments. I usually recommend starting with Level 1 so leadership can see the value, then moving to Level 4 or 5 for a firm-wide rollout with proper governance from the start.

Ethics is part of every session

Every session covers confidentiality, competence requirements, guidance from the Barreau du Québec, the Chambre des notaires, and other Canadian law societies, privacy law (Law 25, PIPEDA), and client consent. It comes up naturally, as part of the work, not as a separate lecture. I'll make sure your team understands one key principle from the start: AI gives you a helpful draft, never a final product. That simple habit keeps everything on solid ground.
