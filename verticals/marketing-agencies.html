More to do, same resources

Your friendly guide to AI marketing tools

There are a lot of tools out there, and it's easy to feel overwhelmed. Don't worry. I'll help you figure out which ones are right for your team and show you how to use them with confidence.

AI Copywriting & Content

Paid

Jasper

A writing assistant built specifically for marketing teams. It learns your brand voice, helps with campaign workflows, and makes it easy for your whole team to collaborate. Great if you're producing content across multiple clients.

Freemium

Copy.ai

Perfect for quick wins: ad copy, product descriptions, social posts, email subject lines. The templates make it easy to get started, and you'll be surprised how fast the first drafts come together.

Paid

Writer

If you manage multiple brand voices and need everything to stay consistent, Writer is your friend. It keeps style guides, terminology, and tone on track across your whole team.

Paid

Anyword

Writes copy and then tells you which version is likely to perform best. Really handy for ad teams who want to test smarter, not just test more.

AI Image & Creative

Paid

Midjourney

Beautiful AI-generated images for concept art, ad mockups, and social content. The results are genuinely impressive. It now runs in a web app (no Discord needed), and prompting takes a minute to get used to, but I'll walk you through it.

Freemium

ChatGPT Images

Built right into ChatGPT, so there's nothing extra to set up. Great for quick concept visuals, social graphics, and exploring creative directions on the fly.

Freemium

Adobe Firefly

If your team already uses Photoshop or Illustrator, Firefly fits right in. Adobe says its own Firefly models are designed to be commercially safe, which reduces licensing worries.

Freemium

Canva AI (Magic Studio)

Canva's AI features (image generation, background removal, Magic Write) make it incredibly easy to go from idea to finished social graphic. Probably the friendliest starting point for teams new to AI design.

AI Video & Audio

Paid

Synthesia

Type a script, pick an avatar, and get a polished video. No camera, no crew, no studio. Your team will love how easy it makes client presentations and training content.

Freemium

Descript

Edit video the same way you'd edit a document: just highlight and delete. It automatically removes filler words and makes clip creation a breeze. One of those tools that makes you wonder how you ever worked without it.

Freemium

Runway

Turn text or images into video clips with AI. It's a fantastic playground for creative concepts, great for exploring ideas before committing to a full production.

Freemium

HeyGen

Create personalized videos with AI presenters and even clone your own voice. Perfect for client outreach, onboarding, and any time you want a personal touch without the production overhead.

Freemium

ElevenLabs

Need a professional voiceover? ElevenLabs generates natural-sounding voices for videos, podcasts, and audio ads. No studio booking, no scheduling. Just type and listen.

AI SEO & Content Optimization

Paid

Surfer SEO

Tells you exactly how to improve your content to rank better, with clear scores and suggestions. Plugs into Google Docs and Jasper so you can write and optimize in the same place.

Paid

Clearscope

Analyzes what's already ranking and gives your writers clear, actionable recommendations. Your editorial team will appreciate how straightforward the content grades and keyword suggestions are.

Paid

MarketMuse

Helps you see the big picture: where you have strong content, where the gaps are, and what to write next. It even generates briefs so your writers know exactly what to cover.

Freemium

Frase

A great all-in-one option for smaller teams. It researches what people are searching for, generates briefs, and even helps draft sections, all in one place.

AI Social Media Management

Paid

Sprout Social AI

Manages your social presence and suggests what to post, when to post it, and how your audience is feeling about it. A reliable companion for teams juggling multiple accounts.

Paid

Lately

Feed it a blog post or webinar recording, and it creates social posts that sound like you. It learns your voice over time, which means the more you use it, the better it gets.

Paid

Hootsuite AI

Scheduling with a helpful AI layer: it suggests post ideas, recommends hashtags, and predicts how your content will perform. A solid choice for teams already using Hootsuite.

Freemium

Buffer

Clean, simple, and easy to use. Buffer helps you write captions, schedule posts, and track what's working. Perfect if your team wants something straightforward without a steep learning curve.

AI Analytics & Reporting

Paid

Improvado

Pulls data from over 500 sources and turns it into clear, story-driven reports your clients will actually enjoy reading. Can save a lot of manual reporting time every month.

Paid

Whatagraph

If you're managing reports for multiple clients on different schedules, Whatagraph keeps everything organized and automated. Your account managers will thank you.

Paid

Triple Whale

Helps show which ads are driving revenue for e-commerce clients. The creative performance breakdowns make it easy to see what's working and double down on it.

AI Ad Optimization

Paid

Albert.ai

Like having a tireless campaign manager working around the clock. It handles budgets, targeting, and creative optimization across Google and social so your team can focus on strategy.

Paid

Smartly.io

Takes the guesswork out of creative testing and budget allocation. It runs experiments across platforms and tells you where your money works hardest. Your media buyers will love it.

Paid

Bïrch (formerly Revealbot)

Set your rules and let it manage bids and budgets across Meta, Google, TikTok, and Snapchat. It's like putting your ad optimization on autopilot, with guardrails you control.

General AI & Automation Platforms

Freemium

Claude

My top pick for long-form writing, strategy documents, and brand voice development. The Projects feature remembers your brand context between conversations, which is a real time-saver.

Freemium

ChatGPT

Fantastic for brainstorming, quick drafts, and ad variations. You can even build custom GPTs tailored to your team's specific tasks, like a little marketing assistant that knows your workflow.

Freemium

Microsoft Copilot

If your team lives in PowerPoint, Word, and Excel, Copilot adds AI right where you already work. Great for pitch decks, client presentations, and reports, with no new tools to learn.

Freemium

Zapier

Connects your favorite apps together without any coding. Imagine: publish a blog post and your social posts, email draft, and scheduling all happen automatically. It's easier to set up than you'd think.

Freemium

Make (formerly Integromat)

Like Zapier's more flexible sibling. If your workflows need "if this, then that" logic with multiple steps, Make gives you a visual builder that makes it feel manageable.

Freemium

Gamma

Describe what you need, and Gamma builds a polished presentation or report quickly. A huge time-saver for pitch decks and campaign recaps that need to look great fast.

Level 3 · Most Popular

Brand-Specific AI Configuration

4 hours (half-day) · Configuration workshop · 6-15 participants

This is where AI starts to feel like it's truly yours. We configure the tools around your brand: your voice, your style, your standards. Agencies set up profiles for each client. You'll leave with a content system that sounds like you, not like a robot.

  • Brand voice calibration across AI tools
  • Content template library (blog, email, social, ads)
  • SEO content workflow configuration
  • Marketing stack integration (email, social, CMS)
  • Quality standards and review gates
  • Canadian compliance checks: CASL for email, Law 25 for personal data, Québec French-language rules for ads
  • Agencies: multi-client brand voice profiles

Is this a fit for your team?

Boutique & mid-size agencies

2-50 people. You're nimble and talented, but there are only so many hours in the day. AI helps your team punch above its weight: more output, same great people, way less stress.

Large agencies

50+ people. You want everyone on the same page with consistent quality across teams. I typically start with Level 1 so leadership sees the vision, then bring the whole team along with the Two-day intensive (Level 5).

In-house marketing teams

Every quarter brings more channels and more asks. AI gives your team breathing room: the ability to show up on more platforms and do better work, without the late nights.
