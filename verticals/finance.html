Sound familiar? These are the challenges your team deals with every day.

You're not alone in feeling these frustrations. The good news is that AI can genuinely help with each one, and it's easier to get started than you might think.

Too much time gathering, not enough time thinking

Your analysts are talented, but they're spending a lot of their day pulling and formatting data instead of doing the analysis they were hired for. AI can take over the tedious assembly work so your team gets back to the work that matters.

Compliance paperwork that just keeps growing

Regulatory reports, audit docs, KYC/AML narratives, policy updates: the pile never stops growing. AI is genuinely good at drafting structured compliance documents from your data. I'll show your team exactly how to do it safely and within regulatory boundaries.

Client reports you wish you had more time for

Personalizing quarterly reviews for hundreds of clients or assembling detailed board reports can take weeks. Since the format is consistent and the work is repetitive, AI is a natural fit here, and your team can often learn the basics in a single workshop.

People are already experimenting. Let's make it safe.

Chances are, some of your team members are already trying AI tools on their own. That's a good thing: it means they're curious. The best next step is giving them clear guidelines on data safety, PII handling, and audit trails so they can explore with confidence.

Great tools are out there. I'll help your team find the right ones.

There are a lot of AI tools available, and it can feel overwhelming. I help your team cut through the noise, pick the tools that fit their work, set them up properly, and start using them with confidence.

For Research & Analysis Teams

Paid

AlphaSense

Think of it as a search engine built for finance. Ask questions in plain English and get answers drawn from earnings calls, regulatory filings, and broker research. A favorite among research teams on both sides of the street.

Paid

Visible Alpha

Makes it easy to compare analyst estimates and forecasts side by side. If your team spends time benchmarking models or tracking consensus, this tool does the heavy lifting for you. Now part of S&P Global.

Paid

Daloopa

Pulls financial data straight from annual and quarterly filings and press releases and organizes it into analyst-ready models. If your team is tired of copying numbers from filings by hand, this can help a lot.

Paid

Kensho (S&P Global)

S&P Global's AI analytics suite. Helps your team quickly analyze financial documents, transcripts, and filings, and it plugs right into Capital IQ and the broader S&P data ecosystem you may already use.

Freemium

Kavout

Gives your team AI-powered stock scores and investment insights. Helpful for equity analysis, factor investing, and portfolio optimization, and it connects to your existing platforms through a straightforward API.

Free / Open Source

QuantConnect

An open-source platform for building and testing trading strategies. Great for teams that want to explore algorithmic trading with Python or C#. The free tier includes a cloud IDE and historical data.

For Compliance, Risk & Fraud Teams

Paid

ComplyAdvantage

Helps your compliance team screen for AML and KYC issues faster and with fewer false positives. Monitors sanctions, PEPs, and adverse media in real time.

Paid

Hummingbird

Takes the pain out of AML case work. Helps your team manage investigations, draft suspicious activity reports, and handle regulatory reporting with less manual work and more consistency. Built around US (FinCEN) filing, so Canadian teams reporting to FINTRAC should confirm the fit.

Paid

Sumsub

Handles identity verification, document checks, and transaction monitoring so your team can onboard clients faster and support its compliance work. Trusted across banking, crypto, and fintech.

Paid

Feedzai

Spots fraud in real time across payments, banking, and commerce. What makes it especially useful is that its models learn and adapt as new fraud patterns emerge, so your defenses get smarter over time.

Paid

Sardine

Combines device intelligence, behavioral signals, and transaction monitoring to help catch fraud earlier. Built specifically for banks, fintechs, and crypto exchanges.

Paid

Unit21

A no-code platform your ops team can actually use, with no engineering help needed. Handles case management, rules, and suspicious activity detection in a friendly, visual interface.

For Document-Heavy Workflows

Paid

Ocrolus

Reads bank statements, tax returns, pay stubs, and mortgage docs, then pulls out the data your team needs. Many lenders use it to speed up underwriting.

Paid

Eigen (now part of Sirion)

Pulls key data from contracts, loan agreements, and regulatory filings. The best part? It learns your institution's specific document types, so it gets more helpful the longer you use it.

Paid

Heron Data

Processes bank statements, invoices, and accounting data automatically. If your team handles a high volume of documents every week, this tool can save them real, noticeable time.

For Trading & Investment Teams

Everyday AI & Automation Tools

Freemium

Claude (Anthropic)

Especially good at reading and analyzing long documents. Your team can feed it an entire annual report or 10-K and get useful summaries back. The enterprise tier includes strong data privacy protections for regulated work.

Freemium

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

A great all-around tool for drafting, summarizing, and brainstorming. Your team can also use its data analysis feature for quick number crunching. The business and enterprise tiers offer SOC 2 compliance and don't train on your data by default.

Paid

Microsoft Copilot

AI right inside the tools your team already uses: Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook. Helps with financial model commentary, report assembly, and turning data into presentations. If you're on Microsoft, this is the easiest place to start.

Freemium

Perplexity

Like having a research assistant that always cites its sources. Handy for market research, competitive intelligence, and keeping up with regulatory changes. Free to start, with a Pro tier for deeper dives.

Freemium

Zapier

Connects your apps together so repetitive tasks run automatically: report triggers, data pipelines, and notification workflows. Free for simple automations, paid for more involved setups.

Freemium

Make (formerly Integromat)

Similar to Zapier but with more flexibility for complex workflows. Your team can build multi-step data processing pipelines using a visual, drag-and-drop interface, no coding required.

Compliance is built into everything we do together.

Your team will learn to work with AI within the guardrails that matter most: privacy law (PIPEDA and Québec’s Law 25), AMF and CIRO expectations, the CSA’s guidance on AI in capital markets, OSFI’s model risk guideline E-23 and the AMF’s AI guideline (both in force May 2027), FINTRAC reporting, data residency, and Québec’s Consumer Protection Act on credit. For cross-border activity, SEC/FINRA guidance, SOX, SR 11-7, and GLBA still apply. Privacy and compliance rules are built into each workflow, and we review them with your compliance lead.

Enterprise vs. consumer tools, data classification, audit trails, client disclosure: these aren't extras. They are part of every exercise, every prompt library, and every workflow we build with your team.
