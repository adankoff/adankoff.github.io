Media & Content Production

What if your team could create more of the work they're proud of, with AI handling the busywork?

Your writers, editors, and producers have great ideas. They just need more hours in the day. I'll show your team how to use AI for research, drafting, repurposing, and distribution, so one great piece of content can reach every channel, and your team still has energy left for the creative work that matters.

Sound familiar? These are the challenges I hear about most.

Most media teams I talk to are dealing with some version of these. The good news: AI is genuinely helpful here. Not as a replacement for your talent, but as a way to give your best people their time back.

So much to publish, not enough hands

Your audience is on five platforms, but your team hasn't grown in years. Here's where it gets exciting: AI can help you turn one great article into a newsletter section, social posts, a video script, and podcast talking points, so your writers get more reach without more burnout.

Research eats up the whole day

Your journalists and editors are talented writers, but they spend most of their time gathering background, combing through transcripts, and compiling data before writing a word. AI can shrink that research phase considerably, and even surface sources your team might not have found on their own.

Great content deserves to go further

You put real effort into a feature piece, then only manage to repurpose it two or three ways before the next deadline hits. AI makes it easy to adapt that piece for every channel, matching your voice, format, and platform style, so nothing you create goes to waste.

Post-production doesn't have to be a grind

Transcription, show notes, clip selection, episode metadata: it all adds up. AI can handle transcription in real time, pick out the best clips, write your show notes, and draft platform descriptions. Your producers get to focus on the creative side of production again.

The tools I'll help your team get comfortable with

There are a lot of AI tools out there, and it can feel overwhelming. I've done the sorting for you. In my training, I don't just show your team which buttons to press. I help them find the right tools for their work and build habits that stick.

Writing & Editorial

Paid

Jasper

A writing assistant that learns your brand voice and helps your team produce content faster together. Great if you're putting out a high volume of content and want to keep everyone on the same page.

Paid

Writer

If your publication has a detailed style guide and strict editorial standards, Writer helps make sure every piece of AI-assisted content still sounds like you. Think of it as a style guide that actively watches your back.

Freemium

Wordtune

A handy editing companion that rewrites sentences for clarity, adjusts tone, and tightens (or expands) your text. Editors love it for polishing drafts in a fraction of the time.

Freemium

Grammarly AI

You might already know Grammarly for grammar checks. Now it can also help rewrite, adjust tone, and draft new content. Works right inside the editors and CMS platforms your team already uses.

Paid

Notion AI

If your team already plans in Notion, AI is built right in on the Business and Enterprise plans. It's great for editorial calendars, research databases, meeting summaries, and drafting together, with no new tool to learn.

Freemium

Copy.ai

Really helpful for knocking out social posts, marketing copy, and product descriptions quickly. The workflows feature lets you set up repeatable steps with templates, so the routine stuff practically writes itself.

Video Production

Freemium

Runway

Need a quick visual or some b-roll? Runway can generate video from text or images, remove backgrounds, and more. Production teams use it to fill gaps fast without scheduling a shoot.

Freemium

Descript

This one is a favorite. You edit audio and video just by editing the transcript text. It's that simple. Removes filler words, cleans up sound quality, and handles screen recording too. If your team makes podcasts or video, they'll wonder how they lived without it.

Paid

Synthesia

Creates presenter-style videos from a text script, with no cameras, studios, or on-screen talent needed. It's especially handy for training videos, explainers, and producing localized versions in other languages.

Freemium

Pika

A fun, fast way to turn text or images into short video clips. Great for social-ready content and quick visual experiments when you want to try something creative without a big production effort.

Freemium

CapCut AI

Auto-captions, background removal, and effects, all in one place. The free tier covers most of what you need, and it's become a go-to for teams producing short-form social video.

Freemium

Opus Clip

Drop in a long podcast or interview, and Opus Clip finds the most engaging moments for you. It creates vertical clips with captions, ready to post. A real time-saver for getting your long-form content onto social.

Audio & Podcast

Freemium

ElevenLabs

Want to turn articles into audio, create voiceovers, or produce content in multiple languages? ElevenLabs generates remarkably natural-sounding voice from text. You can even clone your own voice for a consistent feel.

Freemium

Podcastle

Built specifically for podcasters. It handles recording, editing, noise removal, and audio leveling, and you can edit audio by editing text, just like a document. Makes the whole production process feel much more approachable.

Freemium

Adobe Podcast

Recorded in a noisy room? No worries. This free, browser-based tool cleans up audio to sound like it was recorded in a studio. No Adobe subscription needed: just upload and go.

Freemium

Riverside.fm

A great pick for remote interviews and recordings. It captures studio-quality audio locally, then helps you with transcription, finding the best highlights, and generating clips, all in one place.

Image Generation

Paid

Midjourney

Produces stunning editorial illustrations, hero images, and conceptual visuals. There's a bit of a learning curve with prompting, but once your team gets the hang of it, they'll reach for it constantly.

Freemium

ChatGPT Images

Built right into ChatGPT, so you can describe what you need in a conversation and refine as you go. Perfect for quick editorial illustrations, thumbnails, and social graphics, with no design skills required.

Freemium

Adobe Firefly

If copyright matters to your publication (and it should), Adobe says its own Firefly models are trained on licensed content, which makes them a safer choice for published work. It plugs right into Photoshop and other Adobe tools your designers already use.

Free

Stable Diffusion

The open-source option: you can run it on your own machine or use hosted services. It takes a bit more technical setup, but it gives you the most flexibility and control over the results.

Freemium

Canva AI

Canva's AI features make it easy for anyone on your team, even non-designers, to create polished social graphics and visual content. Magic Design, text-to-image, background removal, and smart suggestions all built in.

Transcription

Freemium

Otter.ai

Transcribes interviews, meetings, and conversations in real time, even identifying who's speaking. Everything is searchable afterwards, and it plugs into Zoom and Google Meet so your team barely has to think about it.

Paid

Rev AI

When accuracy really matters, Rev is a common choice for newsrooms. It offers high-quality AI transcription with an option for human review, plus an API if you want to build it into your production workflow.

Free

Whisper (OpenAI)

An open-source option you can run right on your own machine. Nothing leaves your computer, which matters for journalists protecting sources and for anyone handling personal information under Québec’s Law 25 or PIPEDA. Supports 90+ languages, too.

Distribution & Analytics

Paid

Chartbeat

Helps you see what's working in real time: which headlines grab attention, which stories keep readers engaged, and where to focus next. Major newsrooms rely on it to make smarter editorial decisions.

Paid

Parse.ly

Tracks how your content performs across channels and gives you friendly nudges: trending topics to cover, pieces worth promoting, and opportunities your team might have missed.

Paid

Echobox

Takes the guesswork out of social distribution. It figures out the best times to post, picks the right content for each platform, and handles scheduling automatically, so your team can stop clock-watching.

Freemium

Buffer / Hootsuite AI

If you're new to automating social distribution, these are friendly places to start. They suggest posts, pick the best times, and show you what's performing, all without a steep learning curve.

Newsroom Tools

Enterprise

AP AI / Associated Press

The AP has been using AI for earnings reports and sports recaps since 2014. They pioneered a thoughtful approach where AI handles data-heavy reporting and humans focus on investigative and narrative work. It's a great model to learn from.

Enterprise

Bloomberg AI

Bloomberg uses AI for financial news, data analysis, and real-time market reporting. Their custom-trained model shows how powerful AI becomes when you tailor it to a specific domain, something I help teams think through.

General AI & Automation

Freemium

Claude

My top pick for research, long-form drafting, and analyzing interview transcripts. It handles really large documents. You can give it your style guide and a handful of example articles, and it'll learn your publication's voice.

Freemium

ChatGPT

The tool most teams try first, and for good reason. It's great for quick drafts, headline ideas, repurposing, and brainstorming. Image generation is built in, too, making it an easy starting point for anyone on your team.

Freemium

Microsoft Copilot

If your team already lives in Microsoft 365, this is the easiest way to start. AI shows up right inside Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, helping with drafts, data, and presentations without switching tools.

Freemium

Zapier

Connects over 10,000 apps together so things happen automatically. For example: publish an article, and AI-generated social posts get scheduled across all your platforms, with no coding needed. It's easier than it sounds.

Freemium

Make (Integromat)

Like Zapier, but with more flexibility for teams who want finer control. You build workflows visually, connecting AI processing, content routing, and multi-platform publishing like building blocks. I walk your team through it step by step.
