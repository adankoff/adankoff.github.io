E-commerce & Retail

Big catalog, small team? AI can help, and it's easier than you think.

I help e-commerce and retail teams get comfortable with AI tools for product content, marketing, customer service, and operations. You don't need to be technical. You just need a guide who knows the tools and speaks your language.

Sound familiar? These are the challenges AI is great at helping with.

If your team is juggling product copy, campaigns, and customer questions all at once, you're not alone. Here's where AI can take real weight off your shoulders.

Content

Too many products, not enough hours

Writing unique copy for every SKU across your website, Amazon, and social channels is a lot. AI can draft descriptions from your spec sheets in minutes. Your team just reviews and polishes. It's like having an extra pair of hands that never gets tired.

Marketing

Marketing content never stops

Emails, social posts, ad copy, seasonal campaigns: it feels like you're always behind. AI can turn a simple campaign brief into a full content package: emails, social posts, and ad variations, all in your brand voice. Your team stays creative; the busywork shrinks.

Customer Service

Customer questions keep piling up

Order updates, returns, sizing questions: most support tickets follow familiar patterns. AI can handle the routine ones instantly and draft thoughtful responses for the rest. Your customers get faster answers, and your team gets breathing room.

Operations

You have the data. Now make it useful.

Sales reports, campaign stats, inventory numbers: it's all there, but who has time to dig through it? AI can turn your raw data into clear summaries with specific next steps, so your team spends less time in spreadsheets and more time making smart moves.

Great tools are out there. I'll help you find the right ones.

There are a lot of AI tools for e-commerce, and it can feel overwhelming. I don't sell any of them. I just help your team learn the ones that actually fit your workflow, so you feel confident using them on your own.

Product Content & Copywriting

Jasper

Paid

A friendly writing assistant built for marketing teams. It helps you create product descriptions, ad copy, and email campaigns, with controls to keep everything sounding like your brand.

Copy.ai

Freemium

Great for quickly drafting product descriptions, social posts, and ad variations. If your team writes a lot of short-form content, this one can save real time.

Describely

Paid

Built specifically for e-commerce teams. Feed it your product data and it creates descriptions in bulk, formatted for each channel. Connects directly to your platforms, too.

Shopify Magic

Included with Shopify

If you're on Shopify, this is already included. It helps with product descriptions, email subject lines, and storefront content, right inside your admin. No extra setup needed.

Surfer SEO

Paid

Helps your product pages rank better in search. It looks at what's working for top-ranking competitors and gives you clear suggestions to improve your own content.

Hypotenuse AI

Paid

Give it your product details and it writes descriptions in bulk, including SEO metadata. Really handy when you have a large catalog to fill out.

Customer Service & Support

Gorgias AI

Paid

A helpdesk built for online stores. It connects to your Shopify order data so it can answer shipping, returns, and product questions automatically. Your team handles the rest.

Tidio

Freemium

A friendly chatbot and live chat for your store. It handles common questions, suggests products, and tracks orders. There's a free tier so you can try it out.

Zendesk AI

Paid

A great fit for larger teams. It routes tickets to the right person, suggests responses, builds your knowledge base, and even picks up on customer sentiment, so fewer things fall through the cracks.

Richpanel

Paid

Lets your customers help themselves (tracking orders, starting returns, handling exchanges) without needing to contact support. Everyone wins.

Merchandising & Recommendations

Nosto

Paid

Shows each shopper products they're more likely to love, based on what they've browsed and bought. It personalizes search and content automatically. It's like having a personal shopper for every visitor.

Dynamic Yield

Paid

Personalizes product recommendations, content, and offers across your website, email, and app. Built-in A/B testing helps you learn what resonates with your audience.

Clerk.io

Paid

Makes your store smarter over time. It learns from how shoppers browse and search, then automatically surfaces the products they're most likely to buy.

Inventory & Demand Forecasting

Inventory Planner

Paid

Takes the guesswork out of reordering. It looks at your sales patterns, seasonality, and lead times, then tells you what to order and when, so you're not stuck with too much or too little.

Prediko

Paid

Built for Shopify brands. It forecasts demand, helps automate purchase orders, and helps you avoid running out of your best sellers. A real peace-of-mind tool for DTC teams.

Fabrikator

Paid

Helps growing brands stay on top of inventory across warehouses. It forecasts demand and sets reorder points automatically, so you can focus on growing instead of counting stock.

Marketing & Email Automation

Klaviyo AI

Freemium

The go-to email and SMS tool for e-commerce. It writes subject lines, picks the best send times, and optimizes your flows, so your emails are more likely to get opened and clicked.

Attentive AI

Paid

Handles SMS and email with a personal touch. It writes messages, finds the best times to send, and segments your audience, so the right people get the right message.

Google Ads AI / Meta AI

Included with ad spend

Already built into your Google and Meta ad accounts. These tools help you target the right audiences, optimize campaigns, and even draft ad creative at no extra cost beyond your ad spend.

Omnisend

Freemium

Email, SMS, and push notifications with ready-made automation workflows for common e-commerce scenarios. A nice option if you want to get up and running quickly without a lot of setup.

Image & Visual Content

Photoroom

Freemium

Turn a phone photo into a professional product image. It removes backgrounds, adds lifestyle scenes, and gets your visuals marketplace-ready. No photographer or Photoshop skills needed.

Claid.ai

Paid

Makes your product photos look better: sharper, with cleaner backgrounds, and consistent across your whole catalog. If you have a lot of images to process, it handles them in batches.

Canva AI

Freemium

Your team doesn't need to be designers. Canva's AI helps anyone create product graphics, social posts, email banners, and marketing materials that look polished and on-brand.

General-Purpose AI

ChatGPT

Freemium

Your everyday writing partner. Product copy, email drafts, social posts, brainstorming: it handles a little bit of everything and most teams find it becomes part of their daily routine.

Claude

Freemium

Especially good at brand voice work and longer-form content, things like policy docs, strategy memos, and keeping tone consistent across a large catalog. A thoughtful collaborator.

Microsoft Copilot

Paid

If your team already uses Microsoft tools, this adds AI right inside Excel, Word, and Outlook. Inventory analysis, documentation, customer emails: it all gets a little easier without learning something new.

Automation & Integration

Zapier

Freemium

Connects your tools so they talk to each other, with no coding needed. Set up automations between Shopify, Klaviyo, Gorgias, and hundreds more. AI even helps you build the workflows.

Make (formerly Integromat)

Freemium

Like Zapier, but with room for more complex workflows. If you need multi-step automations, like turning new orders into content tasks or setting up inventory alerts, this is a great pick.

Shopify Flow

Included with Shopify

Already built into Shopify. Automate inventory alerts, customer tagging, order routing, and more, all without leaving the platform you already know. A great place to start.
