About

20 years designing software delivery workflows that scale teams. Now applied to the biggest efficiency shift of a generation.

My job is making AI easy for people who don’t have time to become experts.

You shouldn’t have to read whitepapers, take courses, or spend your Saturday figuring out prompt engineering. That’s what I’m here for. I’ve spent 20 years leading and coaching technology teams at startups, agencies, and scale-ups, translating complicated technology into workflows people can use on Monday morning, whether for a partner at a law firm, a CFO, or a creative director.

How it works: we meet, I learn your business, and I show you where AI saves the most time and money, usually in the first call. Then I teach your team the tools and help you implement the first workflow. In plain English, and fast. No AI background needed.

I run my own business on AI, every day, so I only recommend what actually works.

What I believe

AI is the most powerful efficiency tool ever created, but most people use it wrong

The technology is not the bottleneck. Most organizations fail at AI because they bolt ChatGPT onto existing workflows and call it a strategy. Real results come from redesigning the work itself around what AI can do.

The gap is not the technology. It is knowing how to apply it

Every industry has specific, repetitive knowledge work that AI can handle. The challenge is mapping your actual workflows to the right tools and patterns. That is what I teach.

Every industry has work that AI can transform

Legal document review. Financial reporting. Marketing content. Project coordination. Code review. The patterns are the same across verticals: high-volume knowledge work where consistency and speed matter.

I built the Orchestra Toolkit because I needed to practice what I teach

I do not just advise on AI workflows. I run my entire practice on them. The Orchestra Toolkit is the platform I built to run my own AI workflows. It is how I know what actually works and what does not.
