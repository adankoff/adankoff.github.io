Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

The short version

Who we are: Alex Dankoff is an AI training and consulting practice based in Montreal. I help teams and organizations of every size save time and money with AI.

What we collect: Your name, email, and company when you contact us. Standard server logs (IP address, browser info) when you visit the site.

Why we collect it: To respond to your inquiry. To keep the website running. That is it. We do not collect data to build advertising profiles, sell to third parties, or do anything you would not expect.

AI transparency: We are an AI-native company. AI agents are central to how we operate internally. But your personal data submitted through our website is not processed by AI systems and is not used to train AI models. If this ever changes, we will update our policy and ask for your consent first.

Who we share it with: Nobody, for marketing or advertising purposes. We use standard business tools (hosting, database and sign-in, calendar booking) that process data on our behalf to provide their services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

International transfers: Some of our service providers are based in the United States. We use contractual safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses, data processing agreements) to protect your data during any international transfer.

Cookies and tracking: We do not set our own cookies or use analytics or tracking technologies. The only cookies come from the Calendly booking widget on our contact page. If we add analytics or tracking in the future, we will ask for your consent first.

Data retention: We keep email inquiries for 2 years and Calendly booking records for 1 year after the call, then delete them. Server logs are kept by our host (GitHub) under its own retention policy.

Your rights: You can access, correct, or delete your data at any time. If you are in Quebec, the EU, or California, you have additional rights detailed in the full policy below. To exercise any right, email a.dankoff@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Security: We use HTTPS, access controls, and standard security practices. If a breach happens, we will notify you and the relevant authorities promptly.

Questions? Email a.dankoff@gmail.com.

This policy explains what personal information Alex Dankoff collects, why we collect it, what we do with it, and what rights you have. We wrote it to be read, not to be skipped.

We comply with Quebec's Law 25 (An Act to modernize legislative provisions as regards the protection of personal information, which amends the Act Respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector), the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), and we build toward CCPA/CPRA readiness for when those thresholds apply.

  • A framework for the retention and destruction of personal information
  • Defined roles and responsibilities for personnel throughout the information life cycle
  • A process for handling complaints regarding the protection of personal information

These governance policies are proportionate to the nature and scope of our activities and are available upon request.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for specific, stated purposes. Nothing vague.

  • To respond to your inquiry. If you email us, we use your name, email address, and message to reply to you. That is the point.
  • To schedule and prepare for discovery calls. When you book through Calendly, we use your name and email to set up and prepare for the call.

Consent requirements (Law 25, section 14): Your consent to the collection, use, and communication of your personal information must be clear, free, informed, and given for specific purposes. We request consent separately for each purpose. When consent is requested in writing, the request is presented separately from any other information communicated to you. You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing a.dankoff@gmail.com. Withdrawal of consent applies going forward and does not affect the lawfulness of processing conducted before the withdrawal.

4. AI and Automated Processing

We are an AI-native company. That means we use AI agents extensively in our internal operations: content creation, product development, project management, and more. Transparency about this matters, especially for a company in our space.

Here is the straight picture:

  • Your personal data is not processed by AI systems. Information you send us by email or submit when booking through Calendly is not fed into AI models, not used for automated decision-making about you, and not used to train any AI system.
  • Our AI agents handle internal operations. We use AI agents (primarily powered by Anthropic's Claude) for tasks like drafting content, managing projects, and building our products. These agents work with our business data, not your personal data.
  • No automated decisions are made about you (Law 25, section 12.1). We do not use AI or any other technology to render decisions based exclusively on automated processing of your personal information. If we ever implement automated decision-making that produces decisions with legal or significant effects on you, we will: (a) inform you at or before the time the decision is made, (b) disclose the personal information used, the reasons and principal factors and parameters that led to the decision, and your right to have the information corrected, and (c) provide you the opportunity to submit observations to a person who is in a position to review the decision.
  • If this changes, you will know. If we introduce AI-assisted processing of personal data in the future (for example, AI-assisted inquiry routing or lead qualification), we will update this policy, disclose the change clearly, and obtain your consent before processing your data through AI systems.
  • Our SaaS platform is separate. Client data processed through the Orchestra Toolkit platform is governed by a separate Data Processing Agreement (DPA) signed with each client, not by this website privacy policy.

AI providers we use in our business operations:

  • Anthropic (Claude): our primary AI provider for agent operations

These providers process our internal business content, not your personal information submitted through this website.

EU AI Act (Article 50) transparency: If at any point you interact with an AI system on our website (such as a chatbot or automated response system), we will clearly inform you that you are interacting with AI. Any content on our website that is generated or substantially modified by AI will be disclosed as such.

6. International Data Transfers

We are based in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. Some of the service providers we use are based in the United States. This means your data may be transferred to and processed in the US.

What we do about it:

  • We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and data processing agreements with service providers to ensure your data is protected during international transfers.
  • Canada has been recognized by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection (adequacy decision). Data transfers from the EU to Canada are permitted under GDPR.
  • For transfers from Canada to the US, we rely on contractual safeguards and due diligence on providers' security practices.

For Quebec residents (Law 25, section 17): Before transferring any personal information outside Quebec (including to other Canadian provinces), we conduct a privacy impact assessment that considers:

  • The sensitivity of the information
  • The purposes for which it will be used
  • The protective measures, including contractual ones, that would apply
  • The legal framework applicable in the destination jurisdiction, including the personal information protection principles that apply there

Personal information is only transferred outside Quebec if the assessment concludes that it would receive adequate protection. We enter into written agreements with all recipients outside Quebec that include appropriate safeguards. If you wish to know whether your personal information has been transferred outside Quebec, you may contact us at a.dankoff@gmail.com.

For EU residents (GDPR): Transfers outside the European Economic Area are made using appropriate safeguards under GDPR Article 46, including Standard Contractual Clauses.

GDPR Article 27 (EU Representative): If Alex Dankoff does not have an establishment in the EU but processes EU residents' personal data, we may be required to appoint a representative in the EU under GDPR Article 27, and will do so if that requirement applies.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Current state: We use one third-party cookie on our contact page (Calendly), and nothing else.

Our website is static HTML and CSS. We do not use analytics (no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Fathom), tracking pixels, or social media tracking widgets. We do not set any first-party cookies.

Calendly scheduling embed: Our contact page embeds a booking widget from Calendly (a US-based scheduling service). When you load the contact page, Calendly’s widget loads its own scripts and sets cookies in order to display and manage the booking flow. Calendly may receive your IP address, browser information, timezone, and any information you submit when booking (name, email, answers to booking-form questions). This is required for the booking to function. If you do not want Calendly to load, do not visit the contact page. You can instead email a.dankoff@gmail.com directly. Calendly’s privacy policy: calendly.com/privacy.

One thing to note: We currently load fonts from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com). When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives your IP address and browser information. We did not choose this to track you. It is a standard web font delivery mechanism. We plan to self-host our fonts to eliminate this data transfer entirely.

If we add analytics or tracking in the future:

  • We will update this policy before any tracking goes live.
  • We will implement a cookie consent mechanism that gives you a real choice (not a dark pattern).
  • Non-essential cookies will not be set until you give consent.
  • You will be able to change your preferences at any time.

9. Your Rights

You have real rights over your data. Here they are, organized by what applies to everyone and what is specific to certain jurisdictions.

Everyone

Regardless of where you are, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Access a copy of your information in a readable format
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Delete your information (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Withdraw consent at any time by emailing us

Quebec Residents (Law 25)

In addition to the above, you have the right to:

  • Data portability (section 27): receive your personal information that you provided to us in a structured, commonly used technological format, or request that it be communicated directly to another person or body authorized by law to collect such information. This applies to computerized personal information collected directly from you.
  • De-identification: request that your personal information be de-identified as an alternative to deletion, where appropriate
  • Know about transfers: be informed if your data has been transferred outside Quebec and the safeguards in place
  • Know about automated processing (section 12.1): be informed about any automated decision-making that affects you, including the personal information used, the reasons and principal factors that led to the decision, and your right to have the information corrected. You also have the right to submit observations to a person who can review the decision. (Currently: we do not use automated decision-making.)
  • De-indexing (section 28.1): request that we cease disseminating your personal information or that any hyperlink attached to your name providing access to information be de-indexed, if the dissemination of the information contravenes the law or a court order, or if the conditions for its collection are no longer met
  • Lodge a complaint: file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (CAI) if you believe your rights under Quebec law have been violated

EU Residents (GDPR)

In addition to the universal rights above, you have the right to:

  • Data portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Restrict processing: request that we limit how we use your data while a concern is being resolved
  • Object to processing: object to processing based on legitimate interest (we will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If the CCPA/CPRA applies to us (see note below), you have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information we collect, the sources, and the purposes
  • Delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information, but you can submit a request for the record
  • Limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights

Note: The CCPA/CPRA applies to businesses meeting specific revenue or data volume thresholds. We currently fall below these thresholds but include these rights proactively for transparency.

How to Exercise Your Rights

Email a.dankoff@gmail.com with your request. Under Quebec Law 25 (section 30), access requests should be made in writing and directed to the person responsible for privacy protection. Be specific about what you are asking for.

Cost of access (Law 25, section 33): Access to your personal information is free of charge. Reasonable fees may apply only for the transcription, reproduction, or transmission of the information, in which case we will inform you of the approximate amount before proceeding.

Response timeline:

  • We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days.
  • We will respond substantively within 30 calendar days (Law 25, section 32; PIPEDA; GDPR).
  • If we need more time (complex requests), we will tell you why and give you an updated timeline. Under GDPR, the response period may be extended by up to two additional months (for a maximum of three months total). Under PIPEDA, an additional 30 days may be taken in limited circumstances. Under Quebec Law 25, extensions are granted by the CAI on request, with no fixed statutory maximum.
  • If we refuse an access or rectification request, we will inform you in writing of the reasons for the refusal and of your recourse before the CAI (Law 25, section 32).
  • Failure to respond within 30 days is deemed a refusal under Law 25.

Verification: We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request. Under Law 25 (section 30), you must demonstrate that you are the person concerned (or their representative, heir, or successor). This is to protect your data from unauthorized access, not to make the process harder.

10. Security

We take security measures that are reasonable given the sensitivity of the information, the purposes for which it is used, its quantity, distribution, and medium (Law 25, section 10):

  • Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We use strong passwords, access controls, and secure infrastructure.
  • Service providers are selected with security and privacy standards in mind.

No system is 100% secure. We are honest about that. If a data breach (or "confidentiality incident" under Quebec law) occurs that poses a risk to you:

Immediate response (Law 25, section 3.5): Upon becoming aware of a confidentiality incident, meaning any access, use, or communication of personal information not authorized by law, or any loss of personal information (section 3.6), we take reasonable measures to reduce the risk of injury and prevent new incidents of the same nature.

Notification obligations:

  • Quebec Law 25 (section 3.5): If the incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will promptly notify the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (CAI) and each affected individual. We will inform you of the nature of the information concerned, the circumstances of the incident, the measures we have taken or intend to take, and your recourse.
  • GDPR (Article 33): We will report qualifying breaches to the relevant EU supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of them. Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will also notify you without undue delay.
  • PIPEDA: We will report breaches creating a real risk of significant harm to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC) and affected individuals as soon as feasible.

Incident register (Law 25, section 3.8): We maintain an internal register of all confidentiality incidents, whether or not they require notification to the CAI or affected individuals. This register is kept for a minimum of five years from the date we became aware of the incident and is available to the CAI upon request.

11. Children and Minors

Our services are designed for business professionals: executives, department heads, operations and technology leaders, and other organizational decision-makers.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age.

Quebec Law 25 (section 4.1): Under Quebec law, personal information of a minor under 14 years of age may not be collected without the consent of the person having parental authority or the tutor, unless the collection is clearly for the benefit of the minor. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 14. Since our services are not directed at minors, we have no mechanism for obtaining parental consent, and any collection from a minor under 14 would be inadvertent.

If we discover that we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information to us, contact us at a.dankoff@gmail.com and we will address it immediately.
