Frequently asked questions

Do participants need technical backgrounds?

No. The Introduction, Executive Briefing, and Industry-Specific workshops are designed for any professional. No coding or technical experience required. The AI Workflow Design Lab and Building AI-Powered Operations workshops are more hands-on, but are structured for operational roles, not just engineers. I adjust the depth based on your team’s experience level.

Where are private workshops delivered?

On-site in Montreal, Ottawa, and Toronto (pricing on request: Montreal is local, Ottawa and Toronto include travel). Remote across Canada (and for international clients on request). Custom locations available for groups of 10 or more.

How many participants can attend?

Private workshops are designed for groups of 8-25 participants. Larger groups are split into multiple sessions, and I lead every session personally. The executive briefing works well for groups of 5-15.

What do participants take away?

Every workshop includes practical artifacts: readiness assessments, workflow designs, working prototypes, or strategic roadmaps depending on the program. No theory-only sessions. Participants also get access to relevant Orchestra Toolkit templates they can use immediately.

What industries do you cover?

I run industry-specific programs for law, finance, healthcare, marketing, consulting, engineering, and more: 12 industries and growing. Each program uses your industry’s tools, terminology, compliance requirements, and real workflow patterns. If your industry is not listed, I will build a custom program.
