Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. Private workshops and working sessions are designed for any professional: lawyers, financial analysts, marketing directors, operations managers, executives. You do not need to write code or understand machine learning. The consulting engagements handle the technical implementation for you.

What industries do you work with?

All of them. I run industry-specific training and consulting for law, finance, healthcare, marketing, consulting, engineering, and more. AI workflow improvements apply to any business with repeatable processes, which is every business. The tools and examples change by industry, but the methodology is consistent.

Why are the consulting prices published?

Because pricing transparency respects your time. You should know what an engagement costs before getting on a call. The published prices are real, not “starting at” placeholders. Tier 2 varies by scope, but the range is genuine. No bait and switch.

Who actually does the work?

I do. Alex Dankoff leads every engagement personally. This is not a firm where a partner sells the work and a junior team delivers it. You get 20 years of technology leadership experience applied directly to your project, from training through implementation through ongoing advisory.

What is the Orchestra Toolkit?

It is the software platform I built and use daily to run AI-native workflows. Coordinated agents, task routing, quality gates, audit trails, human oversight. When I build workflows for consulting clients, they run on this platform. It is also available as a standalone subscription for teams that want to operate independently.

Do we need to start with the assessment?

No. If you already have a clear picture of your current state and know you need architecture design or implementation support, you can enter at any consulting tier. Most organizations benefit from the assessment because it surfaces opportunities they had not considered, but it is not a prerequisite. Many start with a working session or a workshop first.

What does the payment structure look like?

Workshops: quoted per engagement. Consulting Tier 1: $15,000 paid upfront on signing. Tier 2: 40% on signing, 30% at mid-point review, 30% on final delivery. Tier 3: monthly billing (Net 15), first and last month due at signing, 3-month minimum. Tier 4: monthly billing (Net 15), first month due at signing, 6-month minimum.

Do you work with remote teams?

Yes. All services can be delivered remotely via video calls, shared documents, and async communication (Slack or email). I am based in Montreal and serve teams remotely across Canada (and international clients on request). On-site delivery available in Montreal, Ottawa, and Toronto (pricing on request).

Can consulting tiers be bundled?

Yes. Clients who sign Tier 2 alongside Tier 1 receive a 10% discount on the Tier 2 fee. Committing to Tier 3 alongside Tier 2 earns a 15% discount on Tier 2. Bundling also keeps scope and context continuous across phases.
